01 — About

I work at the point where a brand's promise has to become something people actually click, hold, or trust with their money. That means visual systems as much as it means interface logic — a rebrand is unfinished if the checkout flow still feels like a different company.

Recent focus has been design systems for finance and healthcare products, where clarity isn't a style choice — it's a requirement. Outside client work, I write occasionally about the difference between decoration and structure in interface design.
